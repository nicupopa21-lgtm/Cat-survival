Support contact

For help with Cat Survival, contact: nicu.popa21@gmail.com. For privacy, deletion, or ad-related support, include your Player Support ID from the in-game Support & Privacy screen when available.

Common troubleshooting

Game does not start

Restart the app. If the issue continues, restart the device and make sure the app is updated to the newest available version.

Sound or music is too low

Check the in-game music/sound settings and Android media volume. If the problem remains, report the device model, Android version, app version, and whether the issue affects music, sound effects, or both.

Screen layout looks wrong

Send a screenshot if possible and include your device model, Android version, display size, and app version.

Progress looks wrong

Report the app version, approximate level, prestige/collapse state, and what happened before the issue appeared.

Rewarded offer or ad issue

Report what you tapped, whether the offer loaded, whether it completed, and whether Cat Grass was granted. Do not include advertising identifiers.

Testing

Test versions may contain bugs or balance issues. Reports can be sent through the bug report page.
