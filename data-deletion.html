No account required

The current version of Cat Survival does not require a user account, login, username, email address, or cloud profile.

Delete local game data

Local save data and settings are stored on your Android device. To delete them:

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Go to Apps and choose Cat Survival.
  3. Open Storage.
  4. Tap Clear data or Clear storage.

You can also uninstall the app to remove local app data from the device.

Reset analytics consent

If you accepted analytics and want to reset that choice before an in-app opt-out setting is available, clear the app's storage/data or uninstall and reinstall the app. When the consent screen appears again, choose deny if you do not want analytics collection.

Request help with data deletion

Because the current game does not use accounts and does not collect names or emails through the app, we usually cannot identify a specific player's analytics events by personal identity.

For support or privacy questions, contact nicu.popa21@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, government ID, payment details, or other sensitive information.
