No account required

Cat Survival does not require a user account, login, username, email address, or cloud profile.

Delete local game data

Local save data and settings are stored on your Android device. To delete them:

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Go to Apps and choose Cat Survival.
  3. Open Storage.
  4. Tap Clear data or Clear storage.

You can also uninstall the app to remove local app data from the device.

Reset or disable analytics consent

If the game version includes an analytics option, you can disable analytics in the Options or Privacy/Data section of the game. If that option is not available in your version, clear the app's storage/data or uninstall and reinstall the app. When the consent screen appears again, choose your preferred analytics option.

After analytics is disabled, the game should stop sending optional Firebase/Google Analytics gameplay analytics from that point onward.

Firebase/Google Analytics data

If analytics consent is accepted, Firebase/Google Analytics may receive automatic app analytics events and the game's custom cat_session_summary gameplay event. Because the game does not use accounts and does not collect names or emails through the app, we usually cannot identify a specific player's analytics events by personal identity.

Some analytics information may be processed in aggregate or according to Firebase/Google Analytics retention and deletion systems. Contact us if you have a privacy or analytics question.

Request help with data deletion

For support or privacy questions, contact nicu.popa21@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, government ID, payment details, or other sensitive information.
