This Privacy Policy explains how Cat Survival handles information in the Android game.

Summary

Cat Survival does not require an account, does not ask for your name, does not ask for your email address, does not use precise location, does not access contacts, and does not use the camera or microphone.

The game stores save data and settings locally on your device. If you accept the gameplay analytics choice, the game may send only a small gameplay summary. The game also includes optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads, with Google ad privacy choices handled separately through Google's consent form when required.

Local game data

The game may save gameplay progress and settings locally on your device so the game can continue between sessions. This may include level progress, upgrades, prestige or collapse progress, unlocked features, music or visual settings, language choice, gameplay analytics choice, Google ad privacy status, and similar game state.

You can delete local game data by uninstalling the app or by using Android system settings to clear the app's storage/data.

Gameplay analytics

Firebase/Google Analytics collection is disabled by default. Cat Survival asks for a separate gameplay analytics choice. If you deny gameplay analytics, optional gameplay analytics should not be sent from that point onward.

If you accept gameplay analytics, the game may send a custom event named session_end_summary. This event may include only these gameplay parameters: analytics_schema_version, session_seconds, max_level_ever, max_prestige_skill_level, and max_collapse.

Gameplay analytics is used to understand game balance, session length, progression difficulty, technical behavior, and whether players are reaching key game milestones. Cat Survival does not use gameplay analytics to personalize ads.

The game does not intentionally send your name, email address, phone number, address, contacts, photos, messages, payment details, save file, skill tree choices, advertising ID, or precise location as gameplay analytics event parameters.

Firebase/Google may process technical service data required to provide analytics services, depending on SDK behavior and app configuration.

Player Support ID

The game creates a random first-party Player Support ID on your device. This ID is shown in the in-game Support & Privacy screen so you can include it in support or deletion requests.

If gameplay analytics is enabled, the game may use a derived non-PII analytics identity based on the Player Support ID. The visible Player Support ID is not sent as a custom gameplay analytics event parameter.

Analytics and ad choices

You can change the Cat Survival gameplay analytics choice from the in-game Options screen when available. You can also reset local choices by clearing the app's storage/data in Android settings or by uninstalling and reinstalling the app.

You can manage Google ad privacy choices from the in-game Support & Privacy screen by tapping Privacy choices when that option is available.

Rewarded ads and Google ad privacy choices

Cat Survival includes optional rewarded ads, such as an offer to receive a small amount of Cat Grass after a completed ad. Rewarded ads are optional. You can play without choosing rewarded ads.

Rewarded ads are provided through Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads. When required, the game shows Google's consent form before ads can be requested. The in-game Support & Privacy screen includes a Privacy choices button so you can manage Google ad privacy choices later when those choices are available for your device or region.

Google ad privacy choices are separate from Cat Survival's gameplay analytics choice. Advertising identity and Google ad consent status are separate from the Player Support ID and gameplay analytics identity.

Sharing of information

Player data is not sold.

Gameplay analytics data is processed by Firebase/Google Analytics as a service provider for analytics and app measurement. Rewarded ads and ad privacy choices are processed through Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads and Google's consent systems.

Purchases, accounts, and cloud saves

The version covered by this policy does not include in-app purchases, user accounts, login, multiplayer chat, or cloud save accounts.

Children's privacy

The game does not intentionally collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support email or bug report, contact us and we will delete it where possible.

Data deletion

See the Data deletion page for steps to delete local game data, reset gameplay analytics consent, manage ad privacy choices, and contact us about data questions.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game changes, when analytics or ad configuration changes, or when legal or platform requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
